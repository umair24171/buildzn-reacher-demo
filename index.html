AI Inbox — Draft, Review, Escalate

A working demo of a draft-only support inbox. The AI reads Reacher's own help pages, writes a reply with its sources attached, and a human decides what happens next. Nothing sends on its own.

● DEMO — No live inbox connected Draft-only mode
🛡️
Human approval is the send button.

Every draft below needs a person to approve it. Refunds, billing disputes, and bug reports are auto-flagged and held for a lead — they sit in “Held for lead”, not in the send queue, until a human escalates them.

1

AI reads the help pages

Each draft is built only from Reacher pages linked in the draft — pricing, sample management, tutorials, and guides.

2

You review the draft + sources

Check the wording, open the source to verify it, edit in place, or ask for a new version.

3

Approve or escalate

Safe questions get sent after approval. Money, billing, and bugs are held for a human lead with full context — and everything is logged.

Escalation rules

Always on
↩️

Refund requests

Any ask for money back, trial refunds, or chargebacks goes to Billing Lead. AI drafts context only — it never promises a refund.

🧾

Billing disputes

Wrong plan charged, double charges, or invoice questions go to Billing Lead with plan and amount attached.

🐞

Bug reports

Stuck automations, failed sends, and broken pages go to Support Engineering with steps to reproduce from the thread.

😠

Angry or at-risk accounts

Threats to cancel or churn, or repeated failures, escalate even if the question itself looks simple.
